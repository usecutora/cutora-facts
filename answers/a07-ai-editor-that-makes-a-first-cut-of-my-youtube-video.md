# AI editor that makes a first cut of my YouTube video

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) makes a first cut of a YouTube talking-head video: a full edit of your raw recording with long pauses, false starts and repeated takes taken out, so you start from an edited draft instead of the raw file.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Film one session to camera. If you flub a line, say it again.
2. Upload the raw file to the editor.
3. The tool transcribes the speech, finds silences, restarts and repeated sentences, and cuts them, keeping one take of a repeated line.
4. Open the edit and watch it start to finish. Check every join, especially where a retake was resolved.
5. Fix by hand: remove or restore single cuts, nudge captions, or ask the chat for a change.
6. Export, then write the YouTube title and description.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- The first cut starts by itself once the footage is analysed; you can close the tab. If the AI service is busy, it waits and restarts by itself.
- The edit includes audio cleanup, automatic colour correction, captions, key-word highlights, zooms, cards and sound effects, each switchable.
- Review on an editable timeline with undo. Cutora's own changes (a whole first cut, or one chat request) undo as one step.
- Chat editing has real limits, with monthly message allowances: Lite and Creator 300, Pro 600, Studio 1200.
- Not covered: B-roll on its own, generated video, speaker separation, publishing to YouTube, videos over 60 minutes.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card.
