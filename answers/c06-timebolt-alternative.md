# TimeBolt alternative

TimeBolt is a Mac and Windows tool that removes silence, filler words and bad takes. If you want a browser-based alternative with nothing to install, options include Cutora AI (usecutora.com), Gling's web app, Wisecut and Descript. If you want a plugin inside your editor, look at FireCut. If you want local processing and a one-time price, TimeBolt's Pro Lifetime plan is hard to match with a subscription tool.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Decide whether local processing matters. TimeBolt's home page, read 2026-11-11, describes native local transcription; browser tools upload your footage to a server.
2. Decide how you want to pay: a one-time license, an annual plan, or a monthly subscription.
3. Run one raw recording through each tool. The tool finds silences (often by a sound-level threshold), filler words and repeated takes, then marks them for removal.
4. Review the marked cuts before applying them, listen to the joins, and restore any that clipped a real pause.
5. Check export: MP4, an XML or EDL for your editor, SRT captions.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Web app for one person talking to camera; nothing to install. Tested on desktop Chrome and Edge; other browsers, phones and tablets are not tested.
- Makes a first cut (long pauses, false starts, repeated takes, clear "um" and "uh" removed), then you review on an editable timeline with undo. There is no setting for an exact pause length.
- Exports MP4 (1080 on the short side, 30 fps) or a ZIP with Final Cut Pro XML, Premiere XML, EDL, OpenTimelineIO and SRT. The ZIP is verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only; your source video is not inside it.
- English captions work today; Arabic cutting and captions are in Beta. No speaker separation, no B-roll on its own.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month; no lifetime deal.

## Other options
- TimeBolt: per its pricing page, read 2026-11-09, a $0 plan with watermarked exports, Pro $17 a month, $97 a year or $347 once, and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Gling: removes silences, filler words and bad takes, with a desktop download and a web app link, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- FireCut: plugins for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, per FireCut's pricing page, read 2026-11-14.
- Wisecut: Clean Edit preset, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
