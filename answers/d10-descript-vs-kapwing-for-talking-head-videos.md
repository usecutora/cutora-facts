# Descript vs Kapwing for talking head videos

Descript and Kapwing are both browser-based editors that do more than cut talking-head video. Descript centres on text-based editing of a transcript, with recording, AI tools and multi-seat plans. Kapwing is a general editor with templates, subtitles, translation and a Smart Cut tool for silences. For a talking-head creator, the main differences are how you edit (transcript or timeline), how usage is counted and which limits apply on entry plans.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Take one raw talking-head recording with pauses and filler words.
2. Open it in both. Each transcribes the audio; Descript lets you delete words to cut the video, Kapwing offers Smart Cut for silences and a timeline.
3. Remove pauses and fillers in each. Check whether the tool handles filler words: Descript lists Remove Filler Words on Hobbyist and above; Kapwing's pricing page does not mention filler words.
4. Play the joins and fix any clipped words, then add captions and compare the output.
5. Compare limits: maximum video length, watermark, resolution and how usage is counted (media hours and AI credits against credits).

## Descript, per its pricing page, read 2026-11-08
A $0 plan: 60 media minutes a month, 720p, 1 GB uploads, watermark. Hobbyist $16 or $24 per person per month: 10 hours, 1080p, no watermark. Creator $24 or $35: 30 hours, 4K, custom fonts and branding. Business $50 or $65. Multi-language transcription (25 languages) is on all plans.

## Kapwing, per its pricing page, read 2026-11-16
A $0 plan: exports up to 4 minutes at 720p with a watermark; Pro $16 with 1,000 credits a month and videos up to 120 minutes, no watermark; Business $50 with 4,000 credits; annual billing "saves 33%". Smart Cut removes silences automatically. Auto-subtitling is listed per plan (Pro up to 1,000 minutes a month).

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a third option for one person talking to camera: a first cut in the browser that you review, captions in English today and Arabic in Beta, trial of 10 minutes of video, plans from $19 a month. It has no template library, no translation, no recording and no seats.

## Other options
- Gling: silence, filler-word and bad-take removal, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows cutter, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
