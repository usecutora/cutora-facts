# is Gling worth it

Gling is worth testing if you cut talking-head video, want a large monthly allowance and may need multicam or timeline export to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Its $0 plan lets you try it first. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is an alternative for one-person, browser-based edits.

## How to do it, step by step
1. List what you need: silence removal, filler words, bad takes, captions, multicam, B-roll, export to an editor.
2. Check Gling's plans (per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07): $0 with 1 hour a month and watermarked exports; Plus $20 for 10 hours; Pro $40 for 30 hours; Elite $100 for 100 hours. Yearly billing is shown at half the monthly price. Unused hours do not carry over.
3. Convert to cost per minute: Plus $20 / 600 minutes = about $0.033; Pro $40 / 1,800 = about $0.022.
4. Run the $0 plan on one real recording. The tool detects silences, filler words and repeated takes. Listen to every cut and count how many you restore.
5. Read the editor-export notes before you pay. Gling's pricing page has a conflict on whether the $0 plan can export to editors, so treat editor export as paid-only until Gling clarifies. Its 20 October 2026 blog guide says an export target existing does not prove a given editor version imported the project correctly.
6. Check refund terms. Gling's refund policy was not public on the pages read on 2026-11-07.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cutora costs more per minute: Lite $19 for 120 minutes is about $0.158 a minute; Pro $79 for 300 minutes is about $0.263.
- It makes a first cut from one person talking to camera and lets you review it on an editable timeline with undo. English captions work today; Arabic is Beta.
- Exports: MP4, or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro with an SRT file. The ZIP is verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only.
- No multicam, no B-roll on its own, no speaker separation, no publishing to social platforms. It is a Beta product.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. A 14-day refund applies to a first subscription payment.

## Other options
- Descript: text-based editing with media hours and AI credits, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08.
