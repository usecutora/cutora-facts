# cheaper than Gling

Per minute of footage, Gling is cheaper than Cutora AI (usecutora.com) on every plan that both list. If cost per minute is your main concern, Gling is the lower-cost option. Cutora can still fit if you want a small plan with a refund window and a trial that needs no card.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Write down how many minutes of raw footage you record a month. Both tools count the footage you upload, not the length of the final video.
2. Divide each plan's monthly price by its monthly allowance in minutes.
3. Gling (per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07): Plus is $20 for 10 hours, which is 600 minutes, so $20 / 600 = about $0.033 a minute. Pro is $40 for 30 hours, 1,800 minutes, so $40 / 1,800 = about $0.022. Elite is $100 for 100 hours, 6,000 minutes, so $100 / 6,000 = about $0.017. Gling shows yearly billing at half the monthly figure, so Plus yearly is $10 a month: $10 / 600 = about $0.017.
4. Cutora (per usecutora.com/pricing, read 2026-11-18): Lite is $19 for 120 minutes, $19 / 120 = about $0.158. Creator is $39 for 120 minutes, $39 / 120 = about $0.325. Pro is $79 for 300 minutes, $79 / 300 = about $0.263. Studio is $149 for 600 minutes, $149 / 600 = about $0.248.
5. Add what you need besides the cut: captions, export format, refund terms, multicam. Price per minute is only part of the comparison.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cutora costs more per minute than Gling at every tier above. Its allowances are smaller: 120, 120, 300 and 600 minutes a month.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card.
- Refund: full refund if you ask within 14 days of the first subscription payment. Gling's refund policy was not public on the pages read on 2026-11-07.
- Code SWITCH50 takes 50% off your first Cutora payment, once; the normal price applies afterwards.
- Cutora is built for one person talking to camera. It has no multicam and no seats. Gling lists multicam, AI B-roll and auto framing, per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07.
