# Wisecut vs Descript

Wisecut is a prompt-driven AI editor that cuts long videos into clips and has a Clean Edit preset for talking-head cleanup. Descript is a broader editor built on text-based editing, with recording, AI tools, avatars and multi-seat plans. For a quick cleanup of one recording they overlap; for team production or recording, Descript covers more.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Name your job: cleanup and clips from a recording you already have, or recording, editing and collaborating in one place.
2. Run the same raw recording through both. Each transcribes the audio and offers cuts of silences, repeats or fillers; you review before export.
3. Compare the editing model. Wisecut works from a prompt ("Wish") and the transcript; Descript edits the video by editing its transcript text.
4. Compare usage units: Wisecut credits (editor 1 credit per processed minute) against Descript media hours and AI credits.
5. Compare caption languages and export formats on each vendor's page.

## Wisecut, per its own pages
- Read 2026-11-11 (pricing standing view): a $0 plan with 30 minutes a month, 480p, exports up to 3 minutes, no watermark; Starter+ 29 US dollars a month billed monthly or 279 a year with 1080p and 300 credits; the top plan 119 US dollars a month or 999 a year with 4K and 1,200 credits.
- Clean Edit removes false starts, repeated takes and long pauses, and keeps one take of a repeated line. Captions list English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian; Arabic is not stated. Export formats and editor handoff are not stated.

## Descript, per its own pages
- Read 2026-11-08: a $0 plan with 60 media minutes a month at 720p; Hobbyist $16 or $24 per person per month with 10 hours; Creator $24 or $35 with 30 hours at up to 4K; Business $50 or $65. It lists 25 transcription languages, Rooms, avatars and voice clones. Refund policy not public on the pricing page.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a separate option for one person talking to camera: a first cut in the browser, an editable timeline, English captions today and Arabic in Beta, trial of 10 minutes of video, plans from $19 a month. It has no team seats, no avatars, no recording and no clips-from-long-video finder.
