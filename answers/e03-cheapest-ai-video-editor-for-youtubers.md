# cheapest AI video editor for YouTubers

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is not the lowest-priced option: Lite is $19 a month. Cheaper or $0 tiers exist, but they differ in what they limit, so compare cost per hour of footage and what is capped.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Count your monthly raw footage in minutes, not finished minutes.
2. List the jobs you need: silence cutting only, or also retakes, captions and exports.
3. Compare units. Some tools count minutes, some hours, some credits; some cap export length or add a watermark.
4. Use yearly prices only if you will keep the tool; check the refund terms.
5. Test two tools on the same clip before paying.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Plans: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month; a year costs ten months ($190, $390, $790, $1,490).
- Lite: 120 minutes of footage a month, 3 Shorts, 300 chat messages, first cut, captions, no watermark.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small mark, no card.
- It does not have a recurring $0 plan, a lifetime deal or pay-per-video pricing.
- One person to camera, up to 60 minutes a project.

## Other options
- AutoCut: Basic (silence removal only) $6.6 a month billed yearly, AI plan $14.9 a month billed yearly, inside Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, per AutoCut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- TimeBolt: Pro $17 a month or $97 a year; a $0 plan with watermarked exports, per TimeBolt's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Gling: $20 a month for 10 hours, or $10 a month billed yearly; $0 plan with 1 hour and watermark, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Wisecut: $0 plan with 30 minutes a month, 480p, exports up to 3 minutes, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Descript: Hobbyist at $16 or $24 a month (the page does not label which is monthly), per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-16.
