# AI rough cut tool for YouTube videos

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) makes the rough cut for YouTube talking-head videos: it takes a raw recording and removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes so you start from an edited draft. It covers one person to camera, not interviews or multi-camera shoots.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record in one session, one person to camera. Say a line again if you flub it; repeats are easier to detect than silent edits.
2. Upload the file. The tool transcribes it and finds dead air, restarts and repeated sentences.
3. Let it cut. A rough cut keeps one take of each repeated line and drops the gaps.
4. Watch the whole draft. Check the join points, restore any line that was cut wrongly, and remove anything it left in.
5. Fine-tune the pacing, captions and emphasis on the timeline, or hand the cut to your main editor.
6. Export, then write the title, description and thumbnail.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- The first cut is a full edit of the raw recording, with captions, audio cleanup, automatic colour correction, zooms and sound effects added; each extra can be switched off.
- It can hand the edit to another editor: a ZIP with Final Cut Pro XML, Premiere XML, EDL, OpenTimelineIO, an SRT file and a cut list. Cuts are frame-exact at 30 fps. Your source video, effects, colour correction and cleaned audio are not in it. Verified in DaVinci Resolve 21; Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro not yet tested by Cutora.
- Limits: one person talking, no speaker separation, projects up to 60 minutes, no B-roll on its own, no publishing to platforms.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card.
