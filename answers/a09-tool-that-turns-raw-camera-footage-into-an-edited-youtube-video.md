# tool that turns raw camera footage into an edited YouTube video

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) turns raw talking-to-camera footage into an edited video: pauses, false starts and repeated takes cut, audio cleaned, colour corrected, captions and zooms added. It does not handle multi-camera shoots, B-roll or generated footage.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Copy the camera file to your computer. Accepted types: MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM, MKV, up to 2 GB per file.
2. Upload it. Large files upload in parts and can be resumed after a refresh or lost connection.
3. The tool transcribes the audio and finds dead air, restarts and repeated lines, then cuts them.
4. Review the timeline, restore wrong cuts, check captions.
5. Export an MP4 (1920x1080 for YouTube, 1080x1920 for Shorts).
6. Upload to YouTube yourself and write the title and description.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Picture: 4K and HEVC files are accepted, but every export is 1080 pixels on the short side at 30 fps. Footage at 24, 25 or 60 fps is converted to 30. HDR is converted to standard Rec.709; HDR output is not offered. Phone rotation flags are applied.
- Each file is transcribed from its own audio; a separate microphone recording or second camera is not lined up with the video.
- A project holds up to 2 files totalling up to 60 minutes.
- No B-roll on its own: you can place your own clip on the second video lane by hand or by chat. It does not generate video, avatars or voices.
- It does not publish to YouTube. No cloud imports (Google Drive, Dropbox).
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card.

## Other options
- Gling: states multicam and separate-microphone support when exporting to DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-07.
- Descript: edit by transcript, exports up to 4K on its Creator plan, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08.
- Clipchamp: browser and Windows editor with silence removal for pauses over 3 seconds, per Microsoft's own support page, read 2026-11-11.
