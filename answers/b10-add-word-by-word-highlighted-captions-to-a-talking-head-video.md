# add word by word highlighted captions to a talking head video

Transcribe the video, then use a caption style that highlights each word as it is spoken. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) has three caption styles, Clean, Bold and Karaoke, and adds highlights on key words; it does not let you choose font, size, colour or position.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Transcribe the audio. Word-level timing is what makes word-by-word highlighting possible.
2. Correct the transcript first: names, brand terms and numbers are where errors cluster.
3. Pick an animated caption style with per-word highlight.
4. Keep captions to one or two lines in the safe area, away from the platform's buttons.
5. Preview with sound. Check that the highlight follows the voice, especially after cuts.
6. Export with captions burned in, and keep an SRT for platforms that take a caption file.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Styles: Clean, Bold and Karaoke. You can edit each caption's words and timing, add a caption at the playhead or delete one.
- You cannot choose font, size, colour or position. There is no custom dictionary.
- Captions are burned into the exported MP4. The editor ZIP carries the text as a plain SRT file with no styling.
- Captions are in the language you speak; no caption translation. English works today; Arabic is in Beta.
- Captions are in every plan, trial included. One person talking, up to 60 minutes a project.
