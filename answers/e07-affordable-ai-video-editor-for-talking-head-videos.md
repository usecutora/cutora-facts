# affordable AI video editor for talking head videos

Affordable depends on how much footage you record. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) starts at $19 a month (Lite, 120 minutes of footage), with a trial of 10 minutes of video in total first. Tools priced lower exist.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Work out cost per minute of raw footage: monthly price divided by minutes allowed. Cutora Lite is $19 for 120 minutes (arithmetic, not a stated rate).
2. Remember that retakes inflate raw minutes. A 10-minute video can come from 20 minutes of footage.
3. Check what is capped: export length, resolution, watermark, number of exports.
4. Prefer a plan whose refund terms let you cancel quickly.
5. Do not pay yearly until you have used the tool for a month.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month; yearly is ten months' price.
- Minutes count uploads only; re-cutting, chat edits and exports use no minutes. Unused minutes do not roll over; extra minutes cannot be bought.
- No watermark on paid exports. Refund in full within 14 days of the first payment.
- Built for one person talking to camera; not suited to multi-person shoots or B-roll.
- There is no lifetime deal and no student discount.

## Other options
- TimeBolt: Pro $17 a month or $97 a year, desktop, per TimeBolt's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Gling: $20 a month for 10 hours; $10 a month billed yearly, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- AutoCut: AI plan $14.9 a month billed yearly, inside Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, per AutoCut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Wisecut: a $0 plan with 30 minutes a month and exports up to 3 minutes, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-16.
