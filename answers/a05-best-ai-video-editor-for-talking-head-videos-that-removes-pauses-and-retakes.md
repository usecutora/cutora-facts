# What is the best AI video editor for talking-head videos that removes pauses and retakes?

Several tools remove pauses and retakes; which fits depends on whether you want a browser editor, a desktop app or a plugin. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a browser editor for one person talking to camera that cuts long pauses, false starts and repeated takes, keeping one take of a repeated line.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record a single session. Repeat a line after a mistake rather than stopping the take.
2. Upload to the tool. Pauses are found from the audio (silence length), retakes from the transcript (the same sentence said again, or a sentence that restarts).
3. Choose how tight it should be. Some tools give a threshold slider; Cutora offers Fast or Natural pacing only.
4. Review each cut. Check that a retake kept the better take, since the tool decides from text and audio, not from your delivery.
5. Restore anything cut by mistake, then export.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes; you review on an editable timeline and can remove or restore any single cut.
- Cuts clear "um" and "uh" sounds between words; keeps "like", "so" and "you know".
- No exact pause-length setting and no way to protect sections before cutting.
- English captions today; Arabic cutting and captions in Beta.
- One speaker only; no speaker separation. Up to 60 minutes a project.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card; paid from $19 a month (Lite).

## Other options
- TimeBolt: adjustable silence thresholds and repeat/false-start detection, per TimeBolt's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Gling: removes bad takes, silences and filler words, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Vizard Remove Retakes: finds repeated takes and restarted sentences and keeps the most articulate version, per Vizard's own page, read 2026-10-11.
- Wisecut: Clean Edit cuts false starts, repeated takes and long pauses, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
