# Kapwing alternative for talking head videos

Kapwing is a general browser editor with a Smart Cut tool for silences. For talking-head videos, alternatives built around cutting speech are Gling, TimeBolt, Wisecut, Descript and Cutora AI (usecutora.com). If you also use Kapwing for templates, dubbing, meme-style content or multi-clip layouts, a talking-head cutter covers only part of that.

## How to do it, step by step
1. List what you use in Kapwing: Smart Cut, subtitles, filler-word removal, templates, resizing.
2. Run one raw talking-head recording through the candidates. The tool transcribes the speech, finds silences, filler words and repeated lines, and proposes cuts.
3. Review the cuts on the timeline, play every join, and restore any cut that clipped a word.
4. Compare what is listed on the pricing page: watermark on the entry plan, maximum video length, and how usage is counted (credits, minutes or hours).
5. Check caption languages and export formats against what you publish.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Takes one person talking to camera and makes a first cut: long pauses, false starts and repeated takes out, clear "um" and "uh" cut, while words such as "like", "so" and "you know" stay in.
- Adds captions (Clean, Bold, Karaoke; no choice of font, size, colour or position), zooms, cards, sound effects, audio cleanup and colour correction, each switchable; edit on a timeline or by chat, which has real limits.
- Projects up to 60 minutes on paid plans, 10 minutes of video in total on the trial. MP4 export at 1080 on the short side, or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro.
- English captions today, Arabic cutting and captions in Beta; no caption translation, no B-roll on its own, no template library.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- Kapwing: per Kapwing's pricing page, read 2026-11-16, a $0 plan with 720p exports, a watermark and a 4-minute maximum video; Pro $16 with 1,000 credits a month and 120-minute videos; Smart Cut removes silences automatically; filler words are not mentioned on that page.
- Gling: removes silences, filler words and bad takes, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows; Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Descript: text-based editing, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
