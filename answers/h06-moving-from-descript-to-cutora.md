# moving from Descript to Cutora

Moving from Descript to Cutora AI (usecutora.com) carries over your original video files only. Descript projects, caption styles and effects do not move; Cutora makes its first cut again from your footage. Cutora does much less than Descript, so check the list below before you switch.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Gather your original recordings (MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM or MKV, up to 2 GB each, up to 2 files per project, projects up to 60 minutes on paid plans). Upload from your device; there is no cloud import.
2. Make a Cutora account with an email. The trial needs no card: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark.
3. Upload a recording. The tool removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes and clear "um" and "uh", and adds English captions. Check every cut on the editable timeline; undo what you disagree with.
4. Export an MP4 (1080x1920 or 1920x1080, 30 fps) or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro with project files, an SRT file and a cut list. Only DaVinci Resolve 21 is verified. The ZIP does not include source video, effects, colour correction or cleaned audio.
5. To subscribe, enter SWITCH50 at checkout: 50% off your first payment, once. For example, Creator at $39 would be $19.50 for that payment, then $39.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Does not do what Descript lists, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08: multiple seats, 4K export, 25 transcription languages, avatars, voice clones, Rooms recording, AI credits and tools. Cutora has single-user accounts, 1080p maximum, English captions (Arabic is Beta), no API, no speaker separation, no B-roll on its own.
- Descript is cheaper per minute on its listed allowances (for example Hobbyist $16 / 600 minutes = about $0.027, against Cutora Lite $19 / 120 minutes = about $0.158).
- 14-day full refund on a first Cutora subscription payment. Descript's refund policy is not public on its pricing page.

## Other options
- Stay with Descript if you need text-based editing across a team or higher export resolution, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08.
