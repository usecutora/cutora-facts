# Cutora vs Descript

Descript fits better if you need text-based editing of any video, recording with guests, multiple seats, 4K export, 25 transcription languages, avatars or voice clones. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) fits a narrower job: a first cut of one person talking to camera in the browser, with long pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed and a timeline you review. Choose by the job.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Take one raw talking-head recording with pauses and a retake.
2. Run it through both tools. Each transcribes the audio; you then remove pauses and fillers, either by editing text (Descript) or by reviewing proposed cuts (Cutora).
3. Review the result at normal speed and compare how many manual fixes you needed.
4. Compare usage units: Cutora counts minutes of uploaded footage a month; Descript counts media hours and AI credits.
5. Export and check the format your next step needs.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut for one person talking to camera; clear "um" and "uh" cut; audio cleanup to -14 LUFS; colour correction; captions in Clean, Bold or Karaoke styles; zooms, cards and sound effects, each switchable. Timeline plus chat edits, which have real limits.
- MP4 at 1080 on the short side, 30 fps, or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro (verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only). Nothing is exported above 1080p.
- English captions today, Arabic in Beta, no caption translation. No recording, no avatars or voices, no seats, no public API, no speaker separation, no B-roll on its own. Beta.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month. 14-day refund on a first payment.

## Where Descript fits better
Per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08:
- Plans for teams: Creator allows 1 to 3 seats and Business up to 5, billed separately.
- Export up to 4K on Creator and Business (Cutora tops out at 1080p).
- 25 transcription languages, against English and Arabic (Beta) for Cutora.
- Rooms recording, AI speech with voice clones, avatars and AI-generated media, none of which Cutora does.
- Hobbyist is $16 or $24 per person per month with 10 media hours; Creator $24 or $35 with 30 hours; a $0 plan has 60 media minutes a month.
- Descript's refund policy is not public on that page.

## Other options
- Gling: silence, filler-word and bad-take removal, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows cutter, Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
