# how much does Cutora AI cost

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) has a trial and four paid plans: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79 and Studio $149 a month, or $190, $390, $790 and $1,490 a year. Prices are in US dollars before tax; tax is added at checkout where it applies.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Estimate how many minutes of raw footage you upload in a month. Cutora counts uploaded footage, and every file counts.
2. Match it to a plan: Lite and Creator 120 minutes a month, Pro 300, Studio 600. A yearly plan gets the same minutes every month; unused minutes do not roll over and extra minutes cannot be bought.
3. Check the extras you need: Shorts a month (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20), chat messages (300, 300, 600, 1200), the publish text (titles, description, chapters) from Creator up, queue priority on Pro.
4. Try the trial on your own video before paying.
5. Pick monthly or yearly. A year costs ten months, about 17% less.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Account with email needed.
- Paid exports carry no Cutora mark. Re-cutting, chat edits, exports, music and sound effects use no minutes.
- Refund in full if you ask within 14 days of your first payment. Cancel any time in Manage billing; the plan stays until the end of the paid period.
- Paid through Polar, the merchant of record. No lifetime deal, no pay-per-video option, no student or non-profit discount, no pause.
- An account is for one person; no seats.
- Cutora's pages do not state how a switch between plans mid-period works; write to hello@usecutora.com before relying on it.
