# Gling vs Wisecut vs TimeBolt for YouTubers

All three remove dead air from talking-head video, but they work differently. Gling is an AI cutter for silences, filler words and bad takes. Wisecut is a prompt-driven editor with a Clean Edit preset and clip tools. TimeBolt is a Mac and Windows app with adjustable thresholds and a filler-word add-on. Match the tool to how you edit and pay.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Pick one real episode of raw footage, 10 to 20 minutes.
2. Run it through each. All three transcribe or analyse the audio, find pauses, and propose or apply cuts.
3. Review the cuts at normal speed. Count how many you had to restore and how clean the joins sound.
4. Compare billing units: Gling hours of media, Wisecut credits, TimeBolt a subscription or one-time license.
5. Check export: MP4 for posting, or XML for Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve if you finish there.

## Comparison from each vendor's own pages
- Gling (pricing read 2026-11-07, home read 2026-11-16): $0 plan 1 hour a month with watermark; Plus $20 for 10 hours; Pro $40 for 30 hours; Elite $100 for 100 hours. Removes silences, filler words and bad takes; MP4/MP3, SRT; Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro integration.
- Wisecut (read 2026-11-11): $0 plan 30 minutes a month at 480p; Starter+ 29 US dollars a month billed monthly or 279 a year; Clean Edit removes false starts, repeated takes and long pauses; captions in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian; export formats and editor handoff not stated.
- TimeBolt (pricing read 2026-11-09, features read 2026-11-11): $0 plan with watermark; Pro $17 a month, $97 a year or $347 once; filler removal (UMCheck) $0.03 per minute; adjustable silence threshold; XML export for the three editors; Mac and Windows; 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Arabic: not stated for Gling or Wisecut on the pages read; TimeBolt's features page names Arabic among its UMCheck languages.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a fourth choice for one person talking to camera, in the browser, in Beta. English captions work today and Arabic is in Beta. It has no clips-from-long-video tool, no desktop app and no multicam. Trial of 10 minutes of video, plans from $19 a month.
