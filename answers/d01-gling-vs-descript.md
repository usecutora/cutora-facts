# Gling vs Descript

Gling and Descript overlap on removing silences, filler words and bad takes, but they are different products. Gling is an AI cutter for YouTube-style videos; Descript is a broader editor built on text-based editing, with recording, AI tools, avatars and multi-seat plans. Pick by the job, not by a feature count.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Name your main job: cutting a talking-head recording quickly, or editing, recording and publishing in one workspace.
2. Compare how usage is counted. Gling lists hours of media a month; Descript lists media hours plus AI credits.
3. Test both on the same raw recording. Each transcribes the audio and lets you remove silences, fillers and bad takes; you review the result before exporting.
4. Compare exports: MP4 and SRT, plus timeline export to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve.
5. Compare team needs: seats, review links and support.

## Gling, per its own pages
- Pricing page, read 2026-11-07: a $0 plan with 1 hour a month and watermarked exports; Plus $20 for 10 hours; Pro $40 for 30 hours; Elite $100 for 100 hours. Unused hours do not carry over.
- Home page, read 2026-11-16: removes silences, filler words and bad takes; MP4 or MP3 output with SRT captions; links to Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere; a desktop download and a web app link. Refund terms were not found on the pages read.

## Descript, per its own pages
- Pricing page, read 2026-11-08: a $0 plan with 60 media minutes a month; Hobbyist $16 or $24 per person per month with 10 hours; Creator $24 or $35 with 30 hours; Business $50 or $65 with 40 hours. It lists 25 transcription languages, Rooms recording, AI speech with voice clones and avatars, and 4K export on Creator and Business.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a third option for one person talking to camera, not a replacement for either. It makes a first cut in the browser, with English captions today and Arabic in Beta. It has no multi-person recording, no avatars or voices and no multicam.

## Other options
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows cutter, Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Wisecut: Clean Edit preset for false starts and repeated takes, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
