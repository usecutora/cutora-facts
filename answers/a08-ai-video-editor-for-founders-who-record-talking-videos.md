# AI video editor for founders who record talking videos

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is made for founders, teachers, coaches and creators who film themselves talking to camera: you record, it makes the first cut, and you review it. It suits solo recordings; it is not a team workspace.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record the update, pitch or explainer in one take-through, one person to camera. Repeat lines you want to redo.
2. Upload the file from your device.
3. The tool transcribes the audio and cuts long pauses, false starts and repeated takes, keeping one take of repeated lines.
4. Review. For founder content, check any number, price or claim in the cut and in the captions by hand.
5. Export a vertical or horizontal MP4, or an editor ZIP if someone else finishes it.
6. Post it yourself; editing tools do not replace your own review before publishing.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut, audio cleanup, automatic colour correction, English captions (Arabic in Beta), highlights, zooms, cards and sound effects; each can be switched off.
- When a line makes a clear claim (a number, a cost or a promise), an Energetic cut opens with a short copy of that line under a title made of its own words; the line is still heard where it belongs.
- One person talking; no speaker separation, no second-camera sync.
- An account is for one person: no seats, shared workspaces, client review links or single sign-on. No public API.
- Does not publish to social platforms or add B-roll on its own. Projects up to 60 minutes.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card. You keep all rights to your footage and exports; videos are not used to train AI models.
