# AI tool that writes YouTube titles and makes a thumbnail and Shorts from my video

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) does all three after the cut: its Publish tab drafts titles and a description, makes a thumbnail picture and cuts Shorts from the video (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20 a month). It does not post anything to YouTube.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Finish the edit first; titles and Shorts come from the final video.
2. Generate several title drafts. Choose one that states the real content and fits in the search result without cutting off.
3. Check the description and chapters against the video. AI drafts can invent details, so read every line.
4. Make the thumbnail: one clear face or object, a few big words, readable at small size. Review the picture before using it.
5. Cut Shorts from the strongest moments, in vertical format. Check each one starts on a clear sentence.
6. Upload and publish on YouTube yourself.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Publish tab: titles, a description, a thumbnail picture, and Shorts, with monthly Shorts counts by plan (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20).
- Per the pricing page, the publish text (titles, description and chapters) is listed from the Creator plan up, along with notes and the brief. Lite lists 3 Shorts a month.
- It does not publish to social platforms or schedule posts.
- It does not generate video, avatars or voices. Vertical export is 1080x1920.
- The trial does not list Shorts or the publish text.

## Other options
- Gling: its page lists title and chapter generators, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-07.
- OpusClip: turns a long video into short clips and can publish to social platforms, per OpusClip's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- FireCut Shorts: browser product, 25 videos a month with clips up to 5 minutes, per FireCut's own page, read 2026-11-14.
- Descript: Create Clips is listed among AI tools from its Hobbyist plan, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08.
