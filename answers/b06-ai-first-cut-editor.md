# AI first cut editor

An AI first cut editor takes a raw recording and returns an edited draft you then refine. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is one for talking-head footage: its first cut is a full edit with long pauses, false starts and repeated takes taken out.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record one session to camera.
2. Upload the raw file. The AI transcribes it and marks dead air, restarts and repeated lines.
3. It cuts them and adds supporting edits (captions, audio cleanup).
4. You review the draft. A first cut is a starting point, so expect to restore a few cuts and fix a few captions.
5. Finish on the timeline, or by chat, or in another editor.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- The first cut starts by itself once footage is analysed, on Cutora's servers; you can close the tab.
- It adds audio cleanup (loudness set to -14 LUFS on export), automatic colour correction, captions (Clean, Bold or Karaoke), highlights, zooms, cards and sound effects. Each can be switched off.
- Finish on the timeline with undo, or ask the chat. Chat has real limits and a monthly message allowance (Lite and Creator 300, Pro 600, Studio 1200).
- Handoff ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro (project files, SRT, cut list). Zooms, cards, highlights and effect sounds do not carry over. Verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only.
- Not for: multi-person shoots, B-roll on its own, generated video, videos over 60 minutes.

## Other options
- Gling: lists bad-take, silence and filler removal with timeline export, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Wisecut: Clean Edit preset in a prompt-based editor, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- RoughCut (Filmit): plugin that orders interview quotes in Premiere or Resolve and does not finish the edit, per Filmit's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- TimeBolt: removes silence, filler words and bad takes for a fast rough cut, per TimeBolt's own page, read 2026-11-11.
