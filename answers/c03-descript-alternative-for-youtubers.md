# Descript alternative for YouTubers

For a YouTuber who talks to camera, the useful Descript alternatives depend on what you do with Descript. For cutting pauses, filler words and retakes there are Gling, TimeBolt, FireCut, Wisecut and Cutora AI (usecutora.com). For podcasts with guests, screen recording with Rooms, or AI avatars, Descript stays broader than any of these.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Split your Descript use into jobs: rough cut, captions, audio cleanup, export, publishing text. Most talking-head YouTubers use the first four.
2. Check your typical raw length. Tools count usage differently: Descript by media hours and AI credits, Gling by hours of media, Cutora by minutes of footage uploaded each month (Creator and Lite 120, Pro 300, Studio 600).
3. Record one real episode and run it through two or three tools. Each transcribes the audio, finds pauses, false starts and repeated takes, and proposes cuts.
4. Review the cuts at normal speed. Restore anything that clipped a breath or a joke, and verify captions word by word.
5. Check how it leaves the tool: an MP4 straight to YouTube, or a project file for Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve if you add B-roll there.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut for one person talking to camera: long pauses, false starts and repeated takes out, clear "um" and "uh" cut, audio cleaned (loudness set to -14 LUFS), colour corrected, captions (Clean, Bold, Karaoke), zooms and on-screen text, all switchable and editable.
- Projects up to 60 minutes; exports are 1080 on the short side at 30 fps. It does not add B-roll on its own (you can place your own clip on a second lane) and does not publish to YouTube.
- From the Creator plan the publish text is listed: titles, description and chapters; Shorts are 3, 5, 10 and 20 a month on Lite, Creator, Pro and Studio.
- English captions work today; Arabic is in Beta. Beta product, single-user accounts.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Plans: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- Descript: Hobbyist $16 or $24 and Creator $24 or $35 per person per month depending on billing, 10 and 30 media hours, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
- Gling: aimed at silence, filler and bad-take removal; Plus $20 and Pro $40 a month, per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07.
- FireCut: Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve plugins, plus a browser tool for Shorts, per FireCut's pricing page, read 2026-11-14.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows; Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
