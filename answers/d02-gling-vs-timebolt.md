# Gling vs TimeBolt

Gling and TimeBolt both remove silences, filler words and bad takes from talking-head video. The main differences on their own pages are how you buy them (Gling by monthly hours, TimeBolt by subscription or one-time license) and where they run (TimeBolt is a Mac and Windows app with local processing; Gling offers a desktop download and links to a web app).

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record or pick one raw video, 10 to 20 minutes, with pauses and a few retakes.
2. Run it through both. Each transcribes the audio, finds silences and fillers, and marks cuts.
3. Compare the review step: TimeBolt highlights fillers and false starts before you apply cuts and lets you exclude a word; Gling lets you adjust the text or remove clips from the timeline.
4. Compare cost for your usage. Gling's limit is hours of media a month; TimeBolt's pricing page states no monthly minute limit on Pro, but filler-word removal (UMCheck) is $0.03 per minute on paid plans.
5. Check export to the editor you use, and test an actual import.

## Gling, per its own pages
- Pricing page, read 2026-11-07: a $0 plan (1 hour a month, watermark), Plus $20 (10 hours), Pro $40 (30 hours), Elite $100 (100 hours); cancel any time in Billing; refund policy not found.
- Home page, read 2026-11-16: removes silences, filler words and bad takes; MP4/MP3 and SRT; Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro integration.

## TimeBolt, per its own pages
- Pricing page, read 2026-11-09: a $0 plan (video only, silence removal, watermark, no XML export); Pro $17 a month, $97 a year or $347 once; Teams $97 per seat per year; 30-day money-back guarantee; no trial of Pro.
- Home page and features, read 2026-11-11: Mac and Windows; UMCheck removes fillers, repeats and bad takes with a 25+ preloaded filler list and custom phrases; XML export for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro; MP4, WAV and SRT output.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a browser editor for one person talking to camera, in Beta. It is not part of this comparison; mention it only if you want a web tool with an editable timeline and chat edits. It does not do multicam, has no desktop app and has no lifetime plan.

## Other options
- FireCut: Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve plugins, per FireCut's pricing page, read 2026-11-14.
- Wisecut: Clean Edit preset, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
