# FireCut vs Gling

FireCut and Gling both cut silences and filler from talking-head video. The difference is form: FireCut's main products are plugins that run inside Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, plus a browser tool for Shorts; Gling is a standalone tool with a desktop download and a link to a web app. If you edit in Premiere Pro or Resolve all day, a plugin keeps you there; if you want to cut first and edit later, a standalone tool is simpler.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Decide where you want the cut to happen: inside your editor or before it.
2. Test both on the same raw recording. Each transcribes the audio and finds silences, fillers and repeats.
3. Review the cuts and listen to the joins.
4. Compare usage units: FireCut source hours a month (plugin plans) or 25 videos of up to 5 minutes (Shorts); Gling hours of media a month.
5. Compare captions languages and export to your editor.

- Premiere Pro plugin: Starter $11, Pro $20, Max $34 a month (lower per-month prices billed yearly). Resolve plugin: a $0 plan with silence removal, Captions $8, Pro $12, Max $59.99. Shorts $14.50 a month. Team $34 per user per month.
- Premiere Pro Pro plan lists transcription 25 hours a month, captions in 50+ languages, filler, profanity and repetition cleanup, chapters, zoom cuts, stock B-roll. Resolve captions list 90+ languages.
- Individual plans show a 7-day trial; cancel any time; refund terms not public on that page.

## Gling, per its own pages
- Pricing, read 2026-11-07: a $0 plan with 1 hour a month and watermark; Plus $20 for 10 hours; Pro $40 for 30 hours; Elite $100 for 100 hours. Cancel any time in Billing; refund policy not found.
- Home, read 2026-11-16: removes silences, filler words and bad takes; MP4/MP3 and SRT; Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro integration. Gling's pages do not state caption languages.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is another standalone option for one person talking to camera, in the browser, in Beta. No plugin form, no multicam, no B-roll on its own. English captions today, Arabic in Beta. Trial of 10 minutes of video, plans from $19 a month.

## Other options
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- AutoCut: Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve plugin, per AutoCut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
