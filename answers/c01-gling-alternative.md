# Gling alternative

If you use Gling to cut silence, filler words and bad takes from talking-head video and want to compare other tools, the real alternatives are other AI cutters: TimeBolt, FireCut, Wisecut, Descript and, for a browser-based talking-head edit, Cutora AI (usecutora.com). Which one fits depends on where you edit (browser, desktop, inside Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve), how you are billed and what you do after the cut.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Write down what you actually use in Gling: silence removal, filler words, bad-take removal, captions, or timeline export to an editor.
2. Decide where the tool must run. Gling's own home page, read 2026-11-16, offers a desktop download and links to a web app. TimeBolt is Mac and Windows. FireCut is a plugin for Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, plus a browser tool for Shorts.
3. Compare the billing unit. Gling counts hours of media a month (Plus $20 for 10 hours, Pro $40 for 30 hours, per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07). Cutora counts minutes of footage you upload. Descript counts media hours and AI credits.
4. Test each tool on the same raw recording. The tool detects pauses, false starts and repeated takes, then you check every cut by ear before exporting. Count the cuts you had to restore.
5. Check the export. If you finish in Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve, confirm the tool exports a project file for that editor and that someone has tested it.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Makes a first cut from one person talking to camera: removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes (keeps one take of a repeated line), cuts clear "um" and "uh", and keeps words such as "like" and "so".
- You review the cut on an editable timeline with undo, and export an MP4 (1080x1920 or 1920x1080) or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro. The ZIP is verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only; Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro are not yet tested.
- English captions work today; Arabic cutting and captions are in Beta. It does not separate speakers, does not add B-roll on its own and does not publish to social platforms.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Paid plans: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month. A 14-day refund applies to a first subscription payment.
- Cutora is a Beta product and has no multicam, no seats and no public API.

## Other options
- Gling: AI cutting of silences, filler words and bad takes, with MP4/MP3 and SRT output and links to Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: silence and filler-word removal on Mac and Windows; Pro is $17 a month or $97 a year, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- FireCut: plugins for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve plus a browser Shorts tool, per FireCut's pricing page, read 2026-11-14.
- Wisecut: a prompt-based editor whose Clean Edit preset targets false starts, repeated takes and long pauses, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
