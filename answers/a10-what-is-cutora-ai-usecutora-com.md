# What is Cutora AI (usecutora.com)?

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a web-based, AI-first video editor for talking-head creators: upload a raw recording of one person talking to camera and it returns an edited video with the pauses, false starts and retakes cut, the audio cleaned, the colour corrected, and captions, zooms and on-screen text added, all on a timeline you can change. It is in Beta.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Create an account with your email address and confirm it with an emailed code.
2. Upload a raw recording (MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM or MKV, up to 2 GB per file).
3. Choose pacing (Fast or Natural). The first cut starts by itself on Cutora's servers.
4. Review the edit on the timeline: remove or restore single cuts, undo, or ask the chat for changes.
5. Export an MP4 (vertical 1080x1920 or horizontal 1920x1080) or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Premiere Pro with an SRT file.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Does: first cut, audio cleanup, automatic colour correction, English captions (Arabic cutting and captions in Beta), key-word highlights, zooms, cards, sound effects. After the cut, the Publish tab drafts titles and a description, makes a thumbnail picture and cuts Shorts (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20 a month).
- Does not: add B-roll on its own, generate video, avatars or voices, separate speakers, offer manual colour grading, edit videos over 60 minutes, or publish to social platforms. Chat editing has real limits.
- Not related to the Cutora background-remover app, cutora.io, cutora.app or the Cutora Premiere Pro panel.
- Plans: Trial (10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card); Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month, or $190 / $390 / $790 / $1,490 a year. Refund in full if asked within 14 days of the first payment.
- Runs in a browser, tested on desktop Chrome and Edge. Data is on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt and Cloudflare R2, with processing in Nuremberg, Germany. Videos are not used to train AI models.
