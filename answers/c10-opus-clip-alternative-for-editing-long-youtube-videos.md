# Opus Clip alternative for editing long YouTube videos

Opus Clip turns long videos into short clips. If your goal is to edit the long video itself (cut pauses, false starts and retakes from a talking-head recording), you want a cutter, not a clipper: Gling, TimeBolt, Descript or Cutora AI (usecutora.com). If your goal is to find highlights and post Shorts, Reels or TikToks from a long recording, a clipping tool fits better than a cutter.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Decide the job: a cleaner long video, or short clips from it. They are different tools.
2. For a cleaner long video, upload the raw recording to a cutter. It transcribes the audio, marks long pauses, false starts and repeated takes, and proposes cuts.
3. Review every cut on the timeline and fix the joins.
4. Add captions and export the full video as MP4, or as a project file if you finish in another editor.
5. For shorts, either use a clipping tool on the finished video or cut them by hand from the timeline. Check how each tool counts usage (credits, minutes or hours) before you upload a 40-minute file.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Edits the long video: first cut of one person talking to camera, projects up to 60 minutes, with captions, zooms and audio cleanup, then MP4 export (1080 on the short side, 30 fps).
- After the cut, the Publish tab drafts titles and a description, makes a thumbnail picture and cuts Shorts from the video (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20 a month). Per /pricing, titles, description and chapters are listed from Creator up.
- It does not publish to social platforms, does not add B-roll on its own and does not separate speakers.
- English captions today; Arabic in Beta.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- Opus Clip: per its own site, read 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-16, you drop a link or upload a long video, AI picks highlights, adds captions and reframes, and can publish to social platforms; the pricing page lists a $0 plan with a watermark and paid plans that offer a trial, and does not state a credit or minute allowance consistently.
- Gling: silence, filler-word and bad-take removal, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows cutter, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Descript: text-based editing, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
