# auto captions for YouTube videos with highlighted words

Generate captions automatically, fix the text, and pick a style that highlights the spoken word. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) does this with Karaoke, Bold or Clean captions burned into the video, plus an SRT file in its editor export.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Auto-transcribe. YouTube's own auto-captions exist, but they are plain text, not styled highlights.
2. Edit the text. Fix proper nouns and numbers.
3. For highlighted words, burn the captions into the video. For YouTube's caption track instead, upload an SRT file in YouTube Studio; an SRT carries text and timing but no styling.
4. Preview at phone size. Captions should not cover the face.
5. Export, upload, and check how YouTube displays them.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Burns captions into the MP4 in Clean, Bold or Karaoke style, and highlights key words.
- The ZIP "Export to your editor" holds an SRT with the caption text as plain text, no styling. Zooms, cards and key-word highlights do not carry over.
- You can edit words and timing, add or delete a caption. You cannot choose font, size, colour or position.
- Captions are in the language you speak; no translation. English works today, Arabic is in Beta.
- Cutora does not upload an SRT or the video to YouTube for you.
