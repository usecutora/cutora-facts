# turn a long talking video into YouTube Shorts automatically

Tools that find strong moments in a long video and cut them into vertical clips can do this. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) cuts Shorts from your video in its Publish tab, with a monthly count by plan: Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Edit the long video first so the source is clean.
2. Pick candidate moments: a complete idea of under a minute, starting on a strong sentence and ending on a clear one.
3. Cut each into 9:16 vertical (1080x1920) and add captions.
4. Watch each clip alone. A clip that needs earlier context will not work as a Short.
5. Export and upload to YouTube yourself, or use a scheduler.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cuts Shorts from the video after the edit, up to the plan's monthly count. Vertical MP4 export is 1080x1920.
- Captions (Clean, Bold, Karaoke), highlights and zooms apply to Shorts like any export.
- It does not post to YouTube or any social platform.
- The source is one person talking to camera, up to 60 minutes a project. The trial does not list Shorts.
- Re-cutting and exports use no footage minutes.
- Check each Short by hand; Cutora's pages state no accuracy figure for moment picking.
