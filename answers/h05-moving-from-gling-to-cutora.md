# moving from Gling to Cutora

Moving from Gling to Cutora AI (usecutora.com) carries over your original video files only. Gling projects and timelines do not move; Cutora makes its first cut again from your footage. Check first that you can live without the things Gling does that Cutora does not.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Gather your original recordings (MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM or MKV, up to 2 GB each, up to 2 files per project, projects up to 60 minutes on paid plans). Upload from your device; there is no cloud import.
2. Make a Cutora account with an email. No card is needed for the trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark.
3. Upload a recording. The tool detects long pauses, false starts and repeated takes (keeping one take of a repeated line) and clear "um" and "uh", and keeps words such as "like" and "so". Review every cut on the timeline; undo any you disagree with.
4. Export an MP4 (1080x1920 or 1920x1080) or a ZIP for your editor with project files, an SRT caption file and a cut list. The ZIP is verified in DaVinci Resolve 21; Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro are not yet tested. It does not include your source video, effects, colour correction or cleaned audio.
5. To subscribe, enter SWITCH50 in the discount code field at checkout: 50% off your first payment, once. For example, Lite at $19 would be $9.50 for that payment, then $19.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- No multicam, no separate-microphone sync, no B-roll on its own, no speaker separation, no publishing to social platforms. Gling lists multicam and AI B-roll, per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07.
- English captions work today; Arabic is Beta. Cutora is a Beta product with no seats and no public API.
- Gling is cheaper per minute: Plus $20 / 600 minutes = about $0.033, against Cutora Lite $19 / 120 minutes = about $0.158.
- 14-day full refund on a first subscription payment.

## Other options
- Stay with Gling if multicam or timeline export to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and Resolve is central, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-07.
