# how to edit a talking head video faster

Fix the footage before you edit, and let software do the repetitive cuts. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) does the repetitive first pass: it removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes, so you review a draft instead of cutting from raw.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record better: a script or bullet outline, a quiet room, and one continuous take. Say a line again after a mistake.
2. Let a tool do the first pass: silence removal, retake removal, captions.
3. Review the draft in one watch-through; note timestamps to fix rather than stopping every few seconds.
4. Fix by hand only what the tool got wrong. Restore wrong cuts, fix captions.
5. Use keyboard shortcuts. In Cutora's editor: Space plays and pauses, J and L move one second, arrow keys move a frame, S splits, Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes.
6. Reuse settings: the same effects style and pacing across a series.
7. Export once, then do titles, description and thumbnail.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut, audio cleanup, colour correction and captions are automatic; zooms, cards, highlights and effect sounds are on by default and can be switched off.
- Re-cutting, chat edits and exports use no minutes, so trying a different pacing costs nothing.
- Publish tab: titles, description, a thumbnail picture and Shorts (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20 a month).
- It cannot save you time on footage it does not handle: multi-person shoots, B-roll, files over 60 minutes. We give no time-saved figure.
