# Gling pricing

Per Gling's own pricing page, read 2026-11-16, Gling has four plans: an entry plan at $0, Plus at $20 a month, Pro at $40 and Elite at $100, with a yearly option at half the monthly figure per month. The page shows a dollar sign without naming the currency.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Count your monthly raw media in hours. Gling counts hours of AI-edited media.
2. Match the hours: 1 ($0 plan), 10 (Plus), 30 (Pro), 100 (Elite) a month. Hours do not carry over.
3. Check export needs. $0-plan exports are watermarked; the page lists no watermark on paid plans.
4. If you need timeline export to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or Resolve, note that Gling's page says the $0 plan does not allow it.
5. Compare monthly against yearly: $10, $20 and $50 a month billed yearly for Plus, Pro and Elite. A total yearly charge is not stated; 12 times the figure is our arithmetic.
