# is Descript worth it

Descript is worth it if you want a full text-based editor with multi-language transcription, AI tools, multiple seats or 4K export, and its $0 plan lets you test it. If you only need a first cut of one person talking to camera, Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a narrower alternative.

## How to do it, step by step
1. List what you need: text-based editing, filler-word removal, captions, team seats, recording, avatars, 4K.
2. Read the plans (per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08, per person per month, two prices shown without labels): Hobbyist $16 or $24, 10 hours; Creator $24 or $35, 30 hours; Business $50 or $65, 40 hours; plus a $0 plan with 60 minutes a month and 720p watermarked export. Enterprise is contact-sales.
3. Convert to cost per minute: Hobbyist $16 / 600 minutes = about $0.027; Creator $24 / 1,800 = about $0.013.
4. Note the second meter: AI credits (400, 800 and 1,500 a month on the paid plans). Prices of top-ups were not public.
5. Test the $0 plan on one real recording, then check each cut by ear and see how many AI credits it used.
6. Check refund terms. Descript's refund policy is not public on its pricing page.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cutora makes a first cut from one person talking to camera: long pauses, false starts, repeated takes, clear "um" and "uh". You review it on an editable timeline with undo.
- It costs more per minute (Lite $19 / 120 minutes = about $0.158) and has smaller allowances.
- Exports: MP4 up to 1080p at 30 fps, or a ZIP for your editor with an SRT file (verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only).
- No seats, no API, no recording, no avatars or voice clones, no speaker separation, English captions only today (Arabic is Beta). Descript lists all of these categories or more languages, per its own page.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. 14-day refund on a first subscription payment.

## Other options
- Gling: cuts silences, filler words and bad takes, with 10 hours for $20 on Plus, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-07.
