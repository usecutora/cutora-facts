# Wisecut alternative

Wisecut is a prompt-based AI editor that turns long videos into clips and has a Clean Edit preset for false starts, repeated takes and long pauses. Alternatives depend on what you use it for: Gling, TimeBolt, FireCut and Descript cut talking-head footage, Eddie AI targets interview rough cuts, and Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a browser editor for one person talking to camera.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Note which Wisecut features you use: Clean Edit, Autocut Silence, captions, reframing, or clips from long videos.
2. Pick candidates by job. For cutting a single talking-head recording, compare Gling, TimeBolt and Cutora. For clips from long videos, look at tools built for clipping.
3. Compare billing. Wisecut sells credits (the editor costs 1 credit per processed minute, per its pricing page); others sell minutes or hours.
4. Run one raw recording through each. The tool transcribes, marks silences, false starts and repeated takes, and proposes cuts.
5. Review every cut and listen to the joins. Check which captions languages are stated, especially if you need Arabic, and which export formats you can get.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut of one person talking to camera: long pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed (one take of a repeated line kept), clear "um" and "uh" cut, with an editable timeline and undo.
- Before cutting you choose Pacing: Fast or Natural. There is no setting for an exact pause length and you cannot mark sections to protect before cutting.
- Adds captions, zooms, cards, sound effects, audio cleanup and colour correction, each switchable.
- English captions work today; Arabic cutting and captions are in Beta. It does not publish to social platforms, does not add B-roll on its own and is not a clip-finder for long videos.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- Wisecut: per Wisecut's own pages, read 2026-11-11, a $0 plan gives 30 minutes a month at 480p; Starter+ is 29 US dollars a month billed monthly or US$279 a year; Clean Edit removes false starts, repeated takes and long pauses; Arabic is not stated on the pages read.
- Gling: silence, filler-word and bad-take removal, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: UMCheck removes fillers and bad takes at $0.03 per minute on paid plans, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-11.
- Descript: text-based editing and multi-seat plans, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
