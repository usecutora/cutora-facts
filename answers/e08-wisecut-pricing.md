# Wisecut pricing

Per Wisecut's own pricing page, read 2026-11-16, Wisecut has a $0 plan (30 minutes a month) and two paid plans, shown at US$16.28 and US$58.28 a month effective when billed yearly. The page ran a Black Friday offer, and it does not list monthly-billed prices.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Count your monthly minutes. Wisecut uses credits: the editor costs 1 credit per processed minute, Autopilot 2 per minute, per its page read 2026-10-11.
2. Match credits: 30 on the $0 plan, 300 on the first paid plan, 1,200 on the higher one.
3. Check export caps: $0 plan exports up to 3 minutes at 480p; the first paid plan up to 60 minutes at 1080p; the higher plan up to 90 minutes at 4K.
4. Read which price you are shown. The figures above are the yearly view with an offer, so re-read the page for the standing price.
5. If you run out of credits, extra packs are listed: 150 for US$25, 480 for US$69, 1,440 for US$189, 3,600 for US$429, valid 12 months.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
We make Cutora AI (usecutora.com); this is context, not a ranking.
- Cutora counts footage minutes, not credits: Lite and Creator 120 a month, Pro 300, Studio 600.
- Prices: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month; extra minutes cannot be bought.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card. Wisecut's page lists no trial of paid plans.
- Projects up to 60 minutes; exports 1080 pixels on the short side.

## Other options
- Wisecut's $0 plan: 4 GB maximum file, no watermark, no Autopilot, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Wisecut features: Clean Edit for false starts, repeated takes and long pauses; Autocut Silence with one slider, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11, does not name Arabic among its caption languages.
