# Eddie AI vs Gling

Eddie AI and Gling both use AI to cut raw footage, but they aim at different footage. Eddie AI describes itself as an AI editor for video editors working mainly with interview footage, with multicam sync and a story-driven rough cut. Gling is a cutter for removing silences, filler words and bad takes from talking-head video. Choose by footage type and how you want to pay.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Identify your footage: one speaker to one camera, or several cameras and several interviewees.
2. Run a short sample through each. Both transcribe the audio, then propose removals of silences, filler words and false starts.
3. Review the proposed cuts before export. Eddie AI states you can review removals before export; Gling lets you adjust the text or remove clips from the timeline.
4. Compare cost model: Eddie AI sells credit packs with no subscription; Gling sells monthly plans by hours of media.
5. Compare exports to your editor and test an import.

## Eddie AI, per its own page
Read 2026-11-16 at heyeddie.ai: builds rough cuts from a story description; syncs multiple camera angles; removes filler words, false starts and awkward silences; adds music, captions and generated B-roll; exports to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve (Avid in beta) and MP4; web, Mac, Windows and iPhone apps. Credit packs: $10 for 1,000 credits, $45 for 5,000, $120 for 15,000, $350 for 50,000, no expiry. New accounts get 500 credits and 2 hours of footage with no card. Retake removal and languages are not stated.

## Gling, per its own pages
Pricing page, read 2026-11-07: a $0 plan with 1 hour a month and watermark; Plus $20 for 10 hours; Pro $40 for 30 hours; Elite $100 for 100 hours. Home page, read 2026-11-16: silences, filler words and bad takes removed; MP4/MP3 and SRT; Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro integration. Multicam and separate-microphone workflows are stated for DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro export.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a third option, only for one person talking to camera. It does not sync a second camera, does not separate speakers and does not generate B-roll. Trial of 10 minutes of video, plans from $19 a month.
