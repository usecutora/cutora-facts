# cheaper alternative to Descript for removing pauses

If pause removal is all you need, a narrower tool can cost less than Descript, but the units differ, so compare against your own footage. Options include TimeBolt, Gling, FireCut, Wisecut and Cutora AI (usecutora.com). If you also use Descript for guests, avatars or translation, a pause remover will not replace it.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Count your monthly raw footage in minutes. A weekly 20-minute video with 30 minutes of raw footage is about 120 minutes a month.
2. Convert each price to that unit. Descript lists media hours (10 on Hobbyist, 30 on Creator); Gling lists hours (10 on Plus, 30 on Pro); Cutora lists minutes (120 on Lite and Creator).
3. Add extras. TimeBolt charges $0.03 per minute for its filler-word add-on on paid plans, per its pricing page.
4. Test pause removal on one recording. The tool measures silence, cuts the pauses longer than its threshold and leaves a short gap; you then review each join.
5. Check that the cheap plan has no watermark and exports what you need.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cuts long pauses, false starts and repeated takes from one person talking to camera. You choose Pacing: Fast or Natural; there is no exact pause-length setting and you cannot mark sections to protect.
- Review on an editable timeline; remove or restore any single cut from the Edits list; undo has no fixed step limit. Re-cutting, chat edits and exports use no minutes.
- Lite is $19 a month for 120 minutes, Creator $39 for 120, Pro $79 for 300, Studio $149 for 600; a year costs ten months. Paid exports have no watermark. Unused minutes do not roll over and extra minutes cannot be bought.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. 14-day refund on a first payment.
- English captions today, Arabic in Beta; no speaker separation.

## Other options
- Descript: Hobbyist $16 or $24 per person per month depending on billing, 10 media hours, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
- TimeBolt: Pro $17 a month or $97 a year, with silence removal on every plan, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Gling: Plus $20 a month for 10 hours, per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07.
- FireCut: the Resolve plugin has a $0 plan with silence removal, per FireCut's pricing page, read 2026-11-14.
