# Descript pricing vs Gling pricing

The units differ, so compare hours and features rather than headline prices. Per their own pages, read 2026-11-16: Gling is $0, $20, $40 and $100 a month for 1, 10, 30 and 100 hours; Descript is $0, Hobbyist $16 or $24, Creator $24 or $35, Business $50 or $65 for 1, 10, 30 and 40 hours.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Count your monthly media in hours.
2. Line up the plans by hours: Gling Plus ($20) and Descript Hobbyist both give 10 hours; Gling Pro ($40) and Descript Creator both give 30.
3. Note Descript's two figures: its page shows two prices per paid plan and does not label which is monthly, so confirm on the checkout.
4. Compare what is inside. Descript also counts AI credits (400 a month on Hobbyist, 800 on Creator, 1,500 on Business, per its page) and sells per-person seats. Gling's page lists a single shared feature set.
5. Compare export needs: Descript lists 720p export on its $0 plan, 1080p on Hobbyist and 4K on Creator and Business; Gling's export resolution is not public.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
We make Cutora AI (usecutora.com); this is context, not a ranking.
- Cutora counts footage minutes: Lite and Creator 120 a month, Pro 300, Studio 600, at $19, $39, $79 and $149 a month.
- Exports are 1080 pixels on the short side, nothing above 1080p.
- One person to camera; no seats or shared workspaces.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card.

## Other options
- Descript: students, educators and non-profits can request special rates through a form, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08.
- Gling: cancel or downgrade any time from Billing, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Descript's $0 plan limits web link export to 1 hour; Descript does not list exports without a watermark on that plan, per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-16.
