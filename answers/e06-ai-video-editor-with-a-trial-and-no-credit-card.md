# AI video editor with a trial and no credit card

Several do. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) has a trial of 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card; it needs an account with your email address.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Read the trial terms before you sign up: the length of the allowance, watermark, resolution cap and whether a card is asked.
2. Create an account. Use your own footage, since a demo clip hides the problems that matter.
3. Test the exact job you need: pauses, retakes, captions, export format.
4. Check the exported file for watermark and quality, and play it on the device you publish from.
5. Only then pay, and check the refund policy first.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports, small Cutora mark, no card.
- It includes the automatic first cut, captions, edit by chat or timeline, audio cleanup, zooms, cards, highlights and sounds.
- Deleting a video does not give trial minutes back.
- After the trial: Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month; a subscription is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the first payment.
- It is not a recurring $0 plan: the allowance is total, not monthly.
