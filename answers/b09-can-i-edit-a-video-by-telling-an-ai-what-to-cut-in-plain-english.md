# can I edit a video by telling an AI what to cut in plain English

Yes, within limits. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) lets you ask its chat for changes such as tighter pacing; for exact cuts you use the timeline and the Edits list.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Make the first cut so there is something to edit.
2. Type the request in plain English, for example "cut the part where I talk about pricing" or "tighten the pacing".
3. Check what it changed. AI tools interpret wording, so a loose request may cut more or less than you meant.
4. Undo if wrong. Rephrase with a time or a quoted phrase from the transcript.
5. For a precise cut, split at the playhead (S) and delete (Delete) on the timeline.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Chat edits are real edits that undo as one step, and they use no footage minutes. Monthly message allowance: Lite and Creator 300, Pro 600, Studio 1200.
- Chat has real limits; Cutora's pages do not list every command it can handle, so test a request before depending on it.
- Before cutting, you cannot mark sections to protect. After cutting, you can remove or restore any single cut from the Edits list.
- Chat can put a clip of your own on the second video lane; it does not add B-roll on its own.
- Works on one person talking to camera, up to 60 minutes a project. English captions today; Arabic in Beta.
