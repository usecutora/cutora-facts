# AI tool that removes pauses and retakes from talking-head video

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is one: it removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes from a talking-head recording and keeps one take of a repeated line. Other tools do part or all of this.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record normally and repeat a line when you make a mistake, rather than stopping. Retakes in one continuous session are what these tools detect.
2. Upload the raw file. The tool transcribes it.
3. Pauses are found from silence in the audio. Retakes are found by comparing the transcript: a sentence said again, or a sentence that starts and restarts.
4. The tool keeps one take of the repeated line. How it chooses (last, most articulate) differs by tool, so check which take survived.
5. Review the cut by hand and restore anything wrongly removed.
6. Export.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Removes long pauses, false starts and repeated takes in the first cut, keeping one take of a repeated line.
- Review on an editable timeline: remove or restore any single cut from the Edits list, undo, or ask the chat for tighter pacing.
- You cannot set an exact pause length or mark sections to protect before cutting.
- One person to camera, recorded in one session; no speaker separation. Up to 60 minutes a project.
- English captions today; Arabic cutting and captions in Beta.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, no card.

## Other options
- Vizard Remove Retakes: keeps the most articulate version of a segment, per Vizard's own page, read 2026-10-11.
- TimeBolt: retake detection keeps the last take on scripted video, false-start detection for unscripted, per TimeBolt's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- Tella: Tella Agent marks mistakes and you accept or decline one by one, per Tella's own changelog, read 2026-11-11.
- Wisecut Clean Edit: reduces repeated lines to one take, per Wisecut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
- AutoCut Repeat: works in more than 80 languages, per AutoCut's own page, read 2026-11-11.
