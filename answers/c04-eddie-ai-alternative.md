# Eddie AI alternative

Eddie AI is built around interview-style rough cuts, multicam and a broad set of editing features, so an alternative depends on your footage. If you film yourself alone talking to camera and want pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed, Cutora AI (usecutora.com) does that narrower job in the browser. If you edit multi-camera interviews, Cutora does not replace Eddie AI.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Say what your footage is: one person to camera, an interview with two cameras, or a long recording to be cut into clips.
2. Match the tool to that. Multicam sync and A-roll logging belong to tools built for them; a single-speaker cleanup belongs to a cutter such as Cutora, Gling or TimeBolt.
3. Compare billing models. Eddie AI sells credit packs; most others sell a monthly plan with a minutes or hours allowance.
4. Run the same raw clip through each tool. The tool transcribes the speech, marks silences, filler words, false starts and repeats, and proposes cuts.
5. Review the removals before export, restore anything wrong, and check the export format your editor needs.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Handles one person talking to camera, recorded in one session. It makes a first cut (long pauses, false starts, repeated takes, clear "um" and "uh"), cleans audio, corrects colour, adds captions, zooms and on-screen text.
- Each video file is transcribed from its own audio. It does not line up a second camera or a separate microphone, does not tell speakers apart, does not add B-roll on its own and does not generate video.
- Export: MP4, or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro (verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only).
- English captions work today; Arabic cutting and captions are in Beta.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- Eddie AI: per Eddie AI's own page (heyeddie.ai), read 2026-11-16, it turns raw interview-style footage into a rough cut from a story description, syncs multiple cameras, removes filler words, false starts and silences with a review before export, exports to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and sells credit packs from $10 for 1,000 credits.
- Gling: silence, filler and bad-take removal, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows cutter with Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro export, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Descript: text-based editing with multi-seat plans, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
