# FireCut alternative

FireCut sells plugins for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve plus a browser tool for Shorts. If you want to leave the plugin model, alternatives are standalone cutters: Gling, TimeBolt, Wisecut, Descript, or Cutora AI (usecutora.com), a web editor for one person talking to camera. If you want AI inside Premiere Pro or Resolve while you edit, a standalone tool is not a substitute.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Check which FireCut product you use: Premiere Pro plugin, DaVinci Resolve plugin or Shorts.
2. Decide if you want the cut to happen inside your editor or before it. A standalone tool cuts first and can hand a project file to your editor.
3. Compare usage units. FireCut counts source hours a month on plugin plans; Cutora counts uploaded footage minutes; Descript counts media hours and AI credits.
4. Run one raw recording through each. The tool transcribes, marks silences, false starts and repeated takes, and proposes cuts.
5. Review the cuts, then check captions languages and export format against what you need.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Browser app, nothing to install, tested on desktop Chrome and Edge. For one person talking to camera in one session.
- First cut: long pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed, clear "um" and "uh" cut. Captions in three styles (Clean, Bold, Karaoke), zooms, cards and sound effects, each switchable; edit on a timeline or by chat, which has real limits.
- Export: MP4 or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro (verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only). It is not a plugin and does not run inside your editor.
- Captions: English today, Arabic in Beta; no caption translation. No B-roll on its own. No public API.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19, Creator $39, Pro $79, Studio $149 a month.

## Other options
- FireCut: per its pricing page, read 2026-11-14, a 7-day trial on individual plans, Premiere Pro plans from $11 a month, Shorts at $14.50 a month, captions in 50+ languages on the Premiere Pro Pro plan.

- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows; Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
