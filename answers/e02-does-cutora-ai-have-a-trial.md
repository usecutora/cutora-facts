# Does Cutora AI have a trial?

Yes. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is in Beta and has a trial that costs nothing: 10 minutes of video in total, in any number of videos, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, and no card. It needs an account with your email address.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Open usecutora.com and create an account with your email address; confirm it with the emailed code. Sign in with an emailed code or Google.
2. Upload a raw recording of one person talking to camera. The 10 minutes are total footage across all your videos.
3. Let the first cut run; review it on the timeline and try the chat.
4. Export up to 10 times; each export carries a small Cutora mark.
5. Decide. A paid plan removes the mark. A subscription is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the first payment.
6. Before you subscribe, download what you need; Cutora's pages do not say what happens to a trial project after you subscribe.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Trial includes the automatic first cut (pauses and retakes removed), captions, edit by chat or timeline, audio cleanup of the dialogue, zooms, cards, key-word highlights and sounds.
- Deleting a video does not give trial minutes back; a second file in the same project counts too.
- Exports are 1080 pixels on the short side, same as paid plans. Trial exports can be used commercially, mark included.
- Limits: trial videos total 10 minutes; one person to camera; no B-roll on its own.
- When the daily limit of new accounts is reached, a new account waits in a queue and is let in later, oldest first.
- Cutora's pages do not say whether a trial export can be re-exported without the mark after subscribing.
