# cheaper than Descript

Per minute of footage, Descript's paid plans work out lower than Cutora AI (usecutora.com) on the figures both list. Descript's pricing page shows two prices per plan and does not say which is monthly, so the arithmetic below shows both. Cutora fits if you want a simpler single-user tool with a trial that needs no card and a refund window.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Note how many minutes of raw footage you record a month. Descript counts all imported or recorded media; Cutora counts the footage you upload.
2. Divide price by allowance in minutes.
3. Descript (per Descript's own page, read 2026-11-08, per person per month): Hobbyist $16 or $24 for 10 hours (600 minutes): $16 / 600 = about $0.027, $24 / 600 = $0.040. Creator $24 or $35 for 30 hours (1,800 minutes): $24 / 1,800 = about $0.013, $35 / 1,800 = about $0.019. Business $50 or $65 for 40 hours (2,400 minutes): $50 / 2,400 = about $0.021, $65 / 2,400 = about $0.027.
4. Cutora (per usecutora.com/pricing, read 2026-11-18): Lite $19 / 120 minutes = about $0.158. Creator $39 / 120 = about $0.325. Pro $79 / 300 = about $0.263. Studio $149 / 600 = about $0.248.
5. Check what else the price buys. Descript also meters AI credits (Hobbyist 400, Creator 800, Business 1,500 a month), which the minutes figure does not include.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Cutora costs more per minute than Descript at every tier above, with smaller allowances (120 to 600 minutes a month).
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Descript also has a $0 plan with 60 minutes a month and watermarked 720p export.
- 14-day full refund on a first subscription payment. Descript's refund policy is not public on its pricing page.
- Code SWITCH50: 50% off your first Cutora payment, once.
- Exports are at most 1080p at 30 fps; Descript lists 4K local export on Creator and Business. Cutora has no seats, no API, no avatars or voice clones, and English captions only today (Arabic is Beta); Descript lists 25 transcription languages.
