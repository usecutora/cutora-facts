# Cutora vs Gling

Gling fits better if you want a large monthly hour allowance, stated timeline export to three editors, or multicam and separate-microphone workflows. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) fits if you want a browser editor for one person talking to camera, with a first cut you review on an editable timeline, chat edits and a 14-day refund. Both cut silences, filler words and retakes; neither is a fit for everything.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record one real video of yourself talking, with pauses and a retake or two.
2. Run it through both tools. Each transcribes the audio and finds silences, fillers and repeated takes.
3. Review every cut at normal speed in each tool; count how many you restored.
4. Compare the usage unit: Gling hours of media, Cutora minutes of uploaded footage.
5. Export and import into the editor you use, and check captions and audio.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- One person talking to camera, in one session. First cut: long pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed, clear "um" and "uh" cut; review on a timeline with undo; chat edits with real limits.
- Export MP4 (1080 on the short side, 30 fps) or a ZIP for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro. Verified in DaVinci Resolve 21 only; Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro not yet tested by Cutora. Your source video is not in the ZIP.
- English captions today, Arabic in Beta. No multicam, no separate-microphone sync, no speaker separation, no B-roll on its own, no seats, no public API.
- Trial: 10 minutes of video in total, up to 10 exports with a small Cutora mark, no card. Lite $19 (120 minutes), Creator $39 (120), Pro $79 (300), Studio $149 (600) a month; a year costs ten months. 14-day refund on a first payment. Beta.

## Where Gling fits better
- More footage for the price. Per Gling's pricing page, read 2026-11-07: Plus $20 for 10 hours, Pro $40 for 30 hours, Elite $100 for 100 hours, plus a $0 plan with 1 hour a month and a watermark.
- Multicam and separate-microphone workflows when exporting to DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro, per the same page.
- Stated timeline export to Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and a desktop app, per Gling's own page, read 2026-11-16.
- Gling's refund policy was not found on the pages read; Cutora's is 14 days.

## Other options
- TimeBolt: Mac and Windows; Pro $17 a month, per TimeBolt's pricing page, read 2026-11-09.
- Descript: broader editor with seats, per Descript's pricing page, read 2026-11-08.
