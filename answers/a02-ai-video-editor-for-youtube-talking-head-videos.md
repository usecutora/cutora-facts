# AI video editor for YouTube talking head videos

Cutora AI (usecutora.com) is a web-based AI video editor for YouTube-style talking-head footage: upload one raw recording and it returns a first cut with long pauses, false starts and repeated takes removed, plus captions and audio cleanup. It does not publish to YouTube.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Record one session of one person talking to camera. Keep the file under 2 GB (MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM or MKV).
2. Upload it to an AI editor. The tool transcribes the audio, finds silences, false starts and repeated sentences, and removes them.
3. Review the cut. Play it through and restore anything it removed by mistake; keep one take of any line you repeated.
4. Add captions and check the words and timing by hand, especially names and product terms.
5. Export for YouTube: horizontal 1920x1080 for the main video, vertical 1080x1920 for Shorts.
6. Add your title, description and thumbnail on YouTube yourself, or use a tool that drafts them.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- First cut, audio cleanup, automatic colour correction, English captions (Arabic in Beta), highlights on key words, zooms, cards and sound effects, all on an editable timeline with undo.
- Captions come in Clean, Bold and Karaoke styles; you cannot choose font, size, colour or position.
- Exports are 1080 pixels on the short side at 30 fps; nothing is exported above 1080p.
- After the cut, the Publish tab drafts titles and a description, makes a thumbnail picture and cuts Shorts (Lite 3, Creator 5, Pro 10, Studio 20 a month). Per the pricing page, titles, description and chapters are listed from Creator up.
- It does not upload to YouTube, add B-roll on its own, generate video, or separate speakers. Projects are up to 60 minutes; the trial is 10 minutes in total.
