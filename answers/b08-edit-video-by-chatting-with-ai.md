# edit video by chatting with AI

Yes, some editors accept plain-language requests and apply the edit. Cutora AI (usecutora.com) has a chat next to its timeline: you ask for a change and it applies it, but chat has real limits, so use the timeline for precise work.

## How to do it, step by step
1. Get a first cut first. Chat suits a draft, not a blank project.
2. Ask for one change at a time, naming what you want: "make the pacing tighter", "remove the zoom at the start".
3. Watch the result in the preview. A chat edit is an edit like any other.
4. If it is wrong, undo it. Then fix it by hand on the timeline, or rephrase.
5. Keep requests specific; vague requests give vague edits.

## What Cutora does and does not do here
- Chat edits use no footage minutes, but there is a monthly message allowance: Lite and Creator 300, Pro 600, Studio 1200 messages.
- Cutora's own changes (a whole first cut, or one chat request) undo as one step. The History panel can undo one chat change on its own if nothing was edited after it.
- Chat can ask for tighter pacing, and it can place a clip of your own on the second video lane.
- It does not add B-roll on its own, generate video, avatars or voices, or edit speakers separately.
- Captions can be edited word by word on the timeline; you cannot set font, size, colour or position.
- The trial includes editing by chat or on the timeline.
